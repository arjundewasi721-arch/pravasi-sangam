🗑️ Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal information by following these steps:

Step 1
Send an email to dewasitechnologies@gmail.com.
Step 2
Use the subject line:
Account Deletion Request - Pravasi Sangam
Step 3
Include your registered mobile number or account email so that we can identify your account. Do not send your password or OTP.
Step 4
We may contact you if additional information is reasonably needed to verify the request.
Step 5
We will process verified deletion requests within the time required by applicable law and communicate any relevant limitations or delays.

Data Deleted and Exceptions

We will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize personal information associated with a verified deletion request, subject to applicable law and technical requirements.

Some information may need to be retained for legal compliance, security, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes. Backups may take additional time to expire according to the applicable retention process.
